\# Problem Statement



\## The Problem



Competitive programmers solve large numbers of problems but often struggle to retain the underlying patterns.



Students and job-seekers preparing for technical interviews frequently forget:



\- Which pattern a problem belonged to

\- How to recognize that pattern in a new, unseen problem

\- Why a particular approach worked, weeks after solving it



\---



\## Limitations of Existing Solutions



\### LeetCode Progress Tracking



These platforms track the number of problems solved, not whether the underlying pattern has actually been retained.



\### Generic Spaced Repetition Apps (e.g. Anki)



General-purpose spaced repetition tools are not tailored to competitive programming and require manual card creation for every pattern.



\### Manual Notes



Manually maintained notes or spreadsheets require significant discipline and do not adapt scheduling to individual performance.



\---



\## Proposed Solution



CP Recall creates a pattern-level memory layer over a user's competitive programming activity.



Instead of tracking raw problem counts, CP Recall tracks mastery of the \*patterns\* behind those problems and schedules reviews using FSRS.



Example:



> "You solved a sliding window problem three weeks ago — here's a new one to test whether you still recognize the pattern."



CP Recall surfaces the right problem at the right time to reinforce long-term retention.
