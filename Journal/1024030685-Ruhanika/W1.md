# W1 — Finalising the Idea & Working on Its Reliability
Objective

To finalise our project idea and evaluate whether it could solve a genuine problem in a practical and reliable way.

Work Done

During the first week, our team finalised the idea of CityServe, a local-services marketplace initially focused on the Thapar ecosystem and nearby areas.

Initially, the idea was broader — a platform where users could find local vendors such as electricians, mechanics, tutors, tailors, photographers, salons, laundry services, etc. However, while discussing the idea, we realised that simply creating a directory of vendors would not provide enough value.

We therefore defined CityServe around two major functions:

Directory/Discovery — helping users find and compare local service providers.
Marketplace — allowing users to actually book services or order products, make payments, track the service/order and provide reviews.

The basic flow became:

Discover → Search → Compare → Book/Order → Pay → Track → Review

Working on Reliability

We also started thinking about how we could make the platform reliable and trustworthy. A major concern with local services is that users may not know whether a provider is genuine, reliable or provides good-quality services.

We identified possible mechanisms such as:

Verification of local service providers
Ratings and reviews
Transparent pricing
Availability information
Service/order tracking
Customer feedback
Admin monitoring

We also realised that starting with an entire city would make validation difficult. Therefore, we considered Thapar as a focused initial market, where students, faculty and staff have recurring local-service requirements and where we can realistically understand user needs.

Key Learning

The main learning from Week 1 was that a project should not be built only around a technical idea. We first need to establish who has the problem, what exactly the problem is, and why users would choose our solution.

We also understood that reliability and trust would be important differentiating factors for CityServe.
