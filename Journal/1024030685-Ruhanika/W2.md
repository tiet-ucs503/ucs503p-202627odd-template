# W2 — Project Proposal & Improvements
Objective

To formally present the CityServe idea, receive feedback, and refine the project based on the suggestions received.

Proposal

In Week 2, we prepared and presented the proposal for CityServe.

We explained the problem of discovering and accessing reliable local services through informal channels such as word of mouth, WhatsApp groups, personal contacts and scattered searches.

We proposed CityServe as a platform that would connect:

Customers ↔ CityServe ↔ Local Service Providers

The initial proposal included services such as:

Electricians
Plumbers
Mechanics
Tutors
Tailors
Photographers
Salons
Laundry
Food and delivery services

We also explained the customer journey from discovering a provider to booking, payment, tracking and reviewing the service.

Improvements Suggested

During the discussion, an important suggestion was to narrow the initial target market instead of trying to build a marketplace for everyone.

The focus was changed towards:

Thapar students, faculty, staff and the surrounding Thapar community.

Another important suggestion was to identify users who have a willingness to pay for premium convenience and reliability, rather than competing purely on price.

For example, a user may be willing to pay slightly more for fresh milk delivered reliably to their doorstep from a verified source. In this case, the additional amount is not only for the product but also for:

Freshness
Trust
Quality
Convenience
Time saved
Doorstep delivery
Improvements Made to the Idea

Based on this feedback, we refined CityServe's positioning from a general local vendor marketplace to a:

Thapar-focused trusted local-services ecosystem.

We also expanded our thinking beyond only traditional service providers. The ecosystem can include everyday requirements such as:

Fresh milk and daily essentials
Tiffin and home-cooked food
Laundry
Tailoring
Rafu and clothing repair
Dyeing
Electricians
Plumbers
Appliance repair
Laptop/phone repair
Salon and grooming
Automobile services
Tutors
Photography and event services

We also distinguished between two types of transactions:

Service Booking:
For services such as electricians, plumbers, salons and repairs.

Product/Delivery Order:
For products such as milk, groceries and tiffin.

Key Learning

Week 2 helped us understand that a strong project is not just about adding more features. Focusing on a specific audience and solving their needs well can make the idea more practical and valuable.

Our project evolved from:

"A marketplace for local vendors"

to:

"A trusted, convenient local-services ecosystem for the Thapar community."

This refinement also gave us a clearer direction for the prototype and MVP that we would work on next.
