### Title: Verification of Vendor Details and Improvement of Dataset Authenticity

**Objective:**  
To improve the reliability, authenticity and accuracy of the vendor dataset used in CityServe, ensuring that users can access relevant and trustworthy information about local service providers.

**Work Done:**
- Reviewed the existing CSV dataset containing information about local businesses and service providers.
- Worked on verifying vendor contact numbers, business names, addresses and service categories using available public business listings and online sources.
- Reviewed the dataset for duplicate entries, incomplete information and inconsistent data formats.
- Focused on identifying vendors relevant to the target audience within and around the Thapar University campus.
- Improved the organization and categorization of vendor information to support efficient searching and filtering.
- Reviewed the integration of the CSV dataset with the backend and database.
- Examined the database import mechanism to ensure that the application could retrieve actual vendor information instead of relying entirely on demonstration data.
- Identified the need for a systematic vendor verification mechanism to maintain data authenticity in the future.

**Challenges Faced:**
- Difficulty in verifying certain contact numbers due to limited publicly available information.
- Inconsistent business information across different online sources.
- Availability of outdated or incomplete vendor details.
- Maintaining consistency between the CSV dataset and database records.

**Learning Outcomes:**
- Understood the importance of data authenticity and validation in real-world marketplace applications.
- Gained practical knowledge of data cleaning, verification and dataset management.
- Understood how reliable vendor information contributes to user trust and application usability.

**Outcome:**  
The dataset was reviewed for accuracy, relevance and consistency, with a focus on improving the reliability of vendor listings and identifying records requiring further verification.

**Future Scope:**  
Introduce vendor self-registration, contact verification, administrator approval and periodic verification of existing business records.
