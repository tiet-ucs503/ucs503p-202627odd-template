# Weekly Progress Report – BharatAssist

## Work Completed This Week

This week, I focused on planning and improving the core intelligence and citizen-assistance capabilities of BharatAssist.

### 1. Smart Government Scheme Eligibility Checker
- Worked on the design and implementation approach for an intelligent scheme eligibility assessment system.
- Planned dynamic collection of user details such as age, income, occupation, and state.
- Focused on matching user profiles with eligibility criteria available in the existing government-services database.
- Designed the result flow to explain why a user may qualify or may not qualify for a particular scheme.
- Included handling for incomplete user information through relevant follow-up questions.

### 2. Chatbot Intelligence and Query Handling
- Focused on improving the chatbot's ability to understand complex and multi-step government-service queries.
- Worked on improving the relevance of service retrieval.
- Planned better handling of ambiguous questions and clarification requests.
- Focused on reducing unrelated service recommendations and unsupported answers.
- Considered follow-up context to make conversations more meaningful.

### 3. Feature Integration Planning
- Analysed how the new eligibility functionality can work with the existing Flask backend, SQLite database, and ChromaDB retrieval system.
- Focused on reusing existing project modules and government-service records.
- Ensured that existing functionality and stored data remain protected during enhancements.

### 4. Testing and Reliability
- Prepared test scenarios for complete and incomplete eligibility profiles.
- Identified edge cases involving different states, income levels, and missing information.
- Focused on ensuring that eligibility results are presented as guidance rather than guaranteed government approval.

## Outcome
Established the technical direction for improving chatbot intelligence and introducing a Smart Government Scheme Eligibility Checker while maintaining compatibility with the existing BharatAssist architecture.
