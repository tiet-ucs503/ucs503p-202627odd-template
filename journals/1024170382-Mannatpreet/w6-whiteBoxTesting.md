# Week 7: White-Box Testing

## Objective

The main objective of this week was to perform white-box testing of the Algo Recall application. I focused on examining the internal code and checking whether the implemented logic was functioning as expected.

## Work Completed

- Performed white-box testing on the application.
- Examined the internal code and logic of different parts of the application.
- Checked the execution of conditions, branches and functions.
- Tested different logical paths to identify possible errors.
- Observed the application’s behavior and checked whether the implemented logic produced the expected results.

## Key Observations

Through this testing, I understood how white-box testing helps identify errors that may not be visible through normal application usage. I also learned the importance of checking the internal flow of code rather than focusing only on the final output.

## Outcome

By the end of the week, I had carried out white-box testing of the application and gained a better understanding of how its internal logic could be examined to identify potential issues.
