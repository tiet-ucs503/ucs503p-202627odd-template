# Week 5: Login and Registration Page Development

**Dates:** 9 November 2026 – 15 November 2026

## Objective

The main objective of the fifth week was to develop the Login and Registration pages of Algo Recall. I focused on creating the frontend forms that would allow users to enter their credentials and interact with the application's authentication interface.

## Work Completed

- Created the **Login page** with input fields for email and password.
- Developed the **Registration page** with the necessary fields for creating a new user account.
- Worked on arranging the form elements and maintaining a consistent design across both pages.
- Added basic form interactions and input handling using React.
- Worked on making the forms simple and easy to use.

## Key Observations

While developing these pages, I understood the importance of keeping authentication forms simple and user-friendly. I also worked on maintaining consistency between the Login and Registration interfaces.

## Outcome

By the end of the fifth week, I had completed the frontend structure of the Login and Registration pages. Both forms were ready from the UI perspective, while their actual authentication functionality and backend integration were to be handled separately.
