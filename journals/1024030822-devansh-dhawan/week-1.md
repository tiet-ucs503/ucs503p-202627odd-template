## Week 1

## Work Done

- Attended the initial project discussion with the team regarding the proposed Digital Hostel Laundry Management System.
- Participated in discussions regarding the project objectives and the process of obtaining the necessary permissions.
- Participated in the process of seeking the required permissions from the concerned authorities.
- Had a phone meeting with the laundry coordinator to understand the practical requirements of the laundry staff and their expectations from the proposed software.
- Discussed the willingness of the laundry staff to adopt the proposed software and how it could be integrated into their existing workflow.
- Gathered initial feedback from the laundry coordinator regarding the requirements and expected functionality of the system.
- Identified an important requirement related to clothes collection: once a student's clothes have been delivered, the system should record the delivery status and prevent the same laundry order from being collected again without proper verification.
- Understood that a reliable verification mechanism is required to ensure that clothes are issued only to the intended student and that a completed laundry order cannot be claimed again.
- Most of the first week was spent obtaining permissions and coordinating with the relevant stakeholders, so limited technical development was carried out.

## Learning

- Learned the importance of gathering requirements directly from the intended users of a software system.
- Gained initial experience in stakeholder interaction and requirement gathering through communication with the laundry coordinator.
- Understood how real-world operational problems can be converted into specific software requirements.
- Learned the importance of maintaining accurate delivery and collection records to prevent duplicate collection of laundry orders.
- Understood that the system should be designed around the practical workflow of the laundry staff as well as the requirements of students.

## Challenges Faced

- A significant amount of time was required to obtain the necessary permissions from the concerned authorities.
- Understanding the existing laundry workflow and identifying the practical requirements of the laundry staff required direct communication with the laundry coordinator.
- A key challenge identified was designing a reliable method of verifying whether a laundry order has already been delivered and collected.
- Due to the permission and coordination process, there was limited time available for technical development during the first week.

## Next Steps

- Document and incorporate the requirements gathered from the laundry coordinator into the project design.
- Discuss possible methods of verifying clothes collection and maintaining accurate delivery status with the team.
- Finalize my specific responsibilities within the project.
- Study the existing project structure and requirements in greater detail.
- Begin technical development and documentation based on the finalized requirements.
